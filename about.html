About the author

Tate Throndson

Pastor, preacher, and author helping people find biblical hope in the middle of real life—especially when the questions are painful and easy answers are not enough.

A shepherd’s voice for hurting people

More than an argument. A pastoral conversation.

Tate Throndson is the senior pastor of Castleview Baptist Church in Castle Rock, Colorado, where he has served since planting the church in 2008. He and his wife, Debbie, have served together in full-time ministry for more than twenty-five years.

Over those years, ministry has meant far more than standing behind a pulpit. It has meant hospital rooms, funeral homes, difficult phone calls, counseling conversations, unanswered prayers, and sitting with people when there was no sentence that could make the hurt disappear.

Answers for a Broken Heart grows out of those moments. It was written for the person who needs something more substantial than a cliché and more personal than a textbook—the person awake at 2:00 a.m. asking questions they never expected to ask.

The goal is not to explain away your pain. It is to help you recognize the God who has been present in it all along.

Tate’s approach is intentionally pastoral and deeply rooted in Scripture. The hard questions are allowed to remain hard. Grief is not hurried. Doubt is not treated as a threat. Anger is not hidden behind religious language. And again and again, the path leads back to Jesus—the God who did not remain distant from suffering, but entered it.

Why this voice?

Pastoral experience. Biblical conviction. No promise of easy answers.

The perspective here is pastoral, biblical, and intentionally careful with questions Scripture does not fully explain. The goal is not to rush pain toward a tidy answer, but to tell the truth and keep pointing toward Christ.

Pastoral

Written by a working pastor.

The aim is the same as a good pastoral conversation: listen carefully, tell the truth, open Scripture, and resist the temptation to rush someone through pain.

Biblical

Scripture sets the boundaries.

The answers are written from a Baptist, evangelical Christian perspective and are intended to be clear about what Scripture says—and careful where Scripture does not give us a private explanation.

Personal

Help before promotion.

You can read the Answers, use the guides, and listen to the resources without buying the book. The site is designed to serve hurting people first.
