You do not have to know what you need

Start here.

If you are hurting and everything on the site feels like too much, choose the sentence that sounds most like where you are right now. You do not need the perfect category. You just need a place to begin.

What feels closest to the truth right now?

Pick one. You can always come back and choose another.

I lost someone or something importantI am grieving.Start with grief, loss, sadness, and the ache that remains after something is gone.I cannot make sense of thisWhy did this happen?Start here if suffering has left you asking what God is doing—or why He allowed it.Faith feels quiet or numbGod feels far away.Start here if you believe, but you cannot seem to feel His presence right now.I prayed and nothing changedI am angry, disappointed, or confused with God.Start here if unanswered prayer has become personal.Someone wounded meI am trying to live with what someone did.Start here for betrayal, bitterness, boundaries, forgiveness, and relational pain.My questions are getting louderI am struggling to believe.Start here for doubt, church hurt, disappointment with Christians, or questions about faith itself.I feel emotionally stuckI do not feel like I am getting better.Start here if pain has begun to harden, isolate, or consume more of your life than you want it to.This is not mainly about meSomeone I love is hurting.Start here if you are trying to help someone else without saying the wrong thing.
