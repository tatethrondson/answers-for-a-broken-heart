You do not have to know what you need yet

Tell me where it hurts.

When your heart is overwhelmed, twenty-four answers can still feel like too many. Start with the sentence that sounds most like what you are carrying today. I’ll point you toward a biblical place to begin.

No test. No perfect category. Just choose the closest one.

Choose the closest sentence

What feels heaviest right now?

You can always come back and choose something else. Pain rarely fits neatly into one box.

I lost someone or something I love

I’m grieving.

The empty chair, the funeral, the diagnosis, the goodbye—or a loss nobody else seems to understand.

Start with grief & loss → I cannot make sense of what happened

I keep asking, “Why?”

You believe God is good, but what happened does not feel good. You need room for the question without a shallow answer.

Start with suffering & why → I cannot feel God the way I used to

God feels far away.

You are praying, trying, waiting—and wondering whether God is present at all.

Start when God feels distant → I prayed and the answer was not what I wanted

I’m angry, disappointed, or confused with God.

You may even feel guilty for admitting it. Scripture has room for honest prayers when faith hurts.

Start with anger & unanswered prayer → Someone hurt me deeply

I’m carrying betrayal, resentment, or a broken relationship.

You need help sorting forgiveness from reconciliation, grace from trust, and healing from pretending.

Start with relational hurt → I am not sure what I believe anymore

I’m struggling with doubt or church hurt.

You have questions about God, faith, Christians, or what happened in a church—and easy answers are not helping.

Start with doubt & faith → I feel low, numb, exhausted, or ashamed of how I feel

I’m struggling emotionally.

If you are a Christian who feels depressed, anxious, or emotionally worn down, begin with a gentle pastoral note.

Read “Can Christians Be Depressed?” → I cannot sort any of this out right now

I just need something true to hold onto tonight.

When your thoughts are loud and your strength is low, begin with seven Scriptures and one simple next step.

If all you can do is read one passage, pray one honest sentence, and make it through the next hour, that is a place to begin.
