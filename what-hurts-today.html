What hurts today?

You don’t need the right words. You don’t need to pretend you’re doing better than you are. Start with the question that sounds closest to what you’re carrying, and begin there.

Start where the pain is

You do not have to solve your whole life tonight.

Some days you need an argument. Other days you need permission to grieve, words for an angry prayer, clarity about forgiveness, or simply reassurance that God has not left. These answers are written to meet the question beneath the pain with Scripture, honesty, and pastoral care.
