Why this book

Some questions only become real when they become personal.

The goodness of God can feel very different beside a hospital bed, after a funeral, or while carrying a prayer that was not answered the way you begged Him to answer it.

Answers for a Broken Heart was written for that moment. It begins with the question the hurt is already asking and brings it honestly into the light of Scripture.

The goal is not to explain away your pain. It is to help you recognize the God who has been present in it all along.

This book is for you if…

You still believe—but you’re hurting.

You do not need the right words. Start with the ache that feels most familiar.

You’re grieving.The loss is real, and “moving on” feels like the wrong phrase.
You keep asking why.You believe God is good, but what happened does not feel good.
You prayed—and the answer hurt.You do not know what to do with disappointment in God.
You’re angry with God.And part of you feels guilty for even admitting it.
Someone wounded you deeply.You are sorting forgiveness from trust, wisdom, and boundaries.
You’re questioning what you believe.Easy answers are no longer enough.

Inside the book

Built to meet a hurting reader where they actually are.

Twenty-four questions let you begin with the one closest to your pain instead of reading from page one.

01 · Honest questions

Start where the hurt actually is.

Grief, suffering, doubt, unanswered prayer, relational wounds, anger, and the questions that rarely show up until life becomes personal.

02 · Biblical answers

Scripture without shallow clichés.

Each answer takes the question seriously, opens the Bible carefully, and leaves room for mystery where Scripture does not give us a tidy explanation.

03 · Pastoral next steps

Truth that helps you keep walking.

The goal is not merely to explain pain. Each chapter moves toward Christ, prayer, wise action, and the next faithful step in front of you.
