Why this book

Some questions only become real when they become personal.

The goodness of God can feel very different beside a hospital bed, after a funeral, or while carrying a prayer that was not answered the way you begged Him to answer it.

Answers for a Broken Heart is a pastor walking beside you through those questions—opening Scripture, refusing shallow clichés, and helping you keep looking toward Jesus.

You do not have to understand everything God is doing in order to keep walking with Him.

Start where it hurts

You do not have to read it straight through.

Begin with the question closest to what you are carrying.

Grief & lossWhen the empty place is still painfully real.
Unanswered prayerWhen you prayed and the answer hurt.
Doubt & questionsWhen easy answers no longer feel big enough.
Anger at GodWhen you are afraid to say what you really feel.
Relational woundsWhen forgiveness, trust, and boundaries feel tangled.
Helping someone elseWhen you want to be present without making the hurt heavier.
