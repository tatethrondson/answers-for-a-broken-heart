Why these are here

Resources should serve before they sell.

This page exists to give hurting people something useful before asking them to buy anything or join anything.

Use a journal. Read an answer. Send a guide to a friend. If it helps someone take one honest, faithful step toward hope, it is doing what it was made to do.
