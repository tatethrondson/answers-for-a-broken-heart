Answer #24 · Coming Home

What if I still have questions after all of this?

You can trust Him with what you still don’t understand.

By Tate Throndson · From Answers for a Broken Heart

The short answer

There will always be another question. Christian faith does not mean pretending every mystery has been settled. It means learning to bring those mysteries to the God who has shown us His heart in Jesus.

You may have prayed, studied, listened, and still be carrying a question that hurts. Perhaps you understand more about God than you once did, but you still don't understand why He allowed something to happen. Having an unanswered question doesn't mean you have failed.

The purpose of this book was never to make every mystery disappear. It was to help you recognize the One you're bringing your questions to.

One minute of encouragement

One Scripture: “Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief.” — Mark 9:24 (KJV)

One prayer: Lord, You know what I still don't understand. Help me look at Jesus instead of letting the unanswered question become the whole story.

One next step: Write down the question you are still carrying. Bring that one question honestly to God today.

Look again at Jesus.

After the resurrection, Thomas heard that Jesus was alive, but his friends' testimony wasn't enough. He wanted to see for himself. Eight days later, Jesus came to him. He didn't give Thomas a lecture explaining every detail of the crucifixion. He invited Thomas to look at His hands.

“Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands ... and be not faithless, but believing.”John 20:27 · KJV (excerpt)

Thomas answered, “My Lord and my God” (John 20:28). He hadn't received answers to every question. He had encountered the risen Christ.

That matters when you're struggling with a question you can't resolve. You don't need to call something terrible good or pretend that the pain has stopped. You can acknowledge the wound and still look toward the Savior who carried wounds of His own.

“I don’t have every answer. But I know where to take the questions.”

Sometimes the next faithful step is simply to tell God the truth, ask Him for what you want, trust Him with what you cannot control, and take the next step in front of you.
